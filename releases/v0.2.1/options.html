Verafy settings

Verafy uses your own Claude API key. It's stored in this browser only and sent only to api.anthropic.com.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. Web search must be enabled for your organization in the Console.
More claims give a fuller score but cost more and take longer.
A full read finds every claim on the page and checks the most central ones first. The rest can be checked one at a time.
Past rulings are saved in this browser only, for up to 200 pages. They're never synced or sent anywhere.
